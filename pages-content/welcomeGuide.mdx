**Important Information**ℹ️

- **Sustainability:**
Bring your own reusable water bottle.
Water dispensers will be available.
- **Cleanliness:**
Please dispose of waste responsibly and help keep the venue clean.
- **ID Verification:**
Carry a valid government-issued ID along with your ticket.
Students must present a valid Student ID.
- **Connectivity:**
Please use mobile data during the conference.
- **Phone Etiquette:**
Keep your phone on silent during sessions.
Take calls outside the track halls.
- **Smoking & Alcohol:**
Strictly prohibited on the premises.
- **Luggage Storage:**
No designated luggage storage is available.
- **Bring Your Own Laptop:**
Participants must bring their own laptops for workshops.
- **Parking:**
Parking is limited at both venues.
Public transport and ride-sharing are strongly recommended.
