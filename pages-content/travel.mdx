### Getting to Hyderabad

- **By Plane - Rajiv Gandhi International Airport**
  - If you're arriving by air, the Rajiv Gandhi International Airport serves as your gateway to Hyderabad. Various Indian airlines operate flights to this airport, including Air India, Air India Express, Indian Airlines, Indigo Airlines, Trujet, and SpiceJet.
  - Transportation options include Pushpak AC buses (24/7 service), prepaid taxis, SHE cabs for women, and app-based cabs like Uber and Ola.

- **By Train - Secunderabad or Hyderabad**
  - For those opting for train travel, Indian Railways offer services to Hyderabad from different parts of India. Major railway stations include Secunderabad, Hyderabad, Kachiguda, and Begumpet. Hyderabad Deccan Station, also known as Nampally Station, is a significant hub for trains to South and North India via Secunderabad.

- **By Car**
  - Hyderabad is well-connected to major cities via highways like NH7 (to Bangalore), NH9 (to Pune/Mumbai), and NH65 (to Vijayawada). The Bangalore-Hyderabad corridor is a four-lane divided highway.

- **By Bus**
  - Hyderabad's extensive bus network, including state government and private buses, connects the city to various parts of Telangana, South India, and Western India. Major bus stations include JBS (Jubilee Bus Station) in Secunderabad and MGBS (Imliban), which holds the distinction of being one of the largest bus stations in the world.

For more comprehensive travel information and options, please refer to the [Hyderabad Wiki Page](https://wikitravel.org/en/Hyderabad#Get_in).

### Getting to the Venue

PyConf Hyderabad 2026 will be hosted across two venues:

- **Conference Venue:** [Engineering Staff College of India](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tMbBxW1wD7RUUyJR8), ESCI Road, Lake, beside Khajaguda, Gachibowli, Hyderabad, Telangana 500032.
- **Workshop Venue:** [EPAM Systems](https://maps.app.goo.gl/xAeQmQQTuSoki6U7A), Gowra Palladium, 12th Floor, Plot Nos. 8A and 8B/1, Survey No. 83/1, Raidurg Village, Serilingampally Mandal, Hyderabad, 500081.

Here are the different ways to reach either venue:

- **By Cab**
  - You can book a cab from the airport, railway station, or any part of the city. Popular cab services like Uber, Ola, Rapido, and Meru are available in Hyderabad.

- **Via Hyderabad Metro**
  - Both venues are best accessible via the Hyderabad Metro 🔵 Blue line. You can get down at the "Raidurg" stop.
  - From Raidurg, you can use cab-sharing services for a quick and convenient ride to either venue.
    - **From the 🔴 Red Line:** If you're arriving via the Red line, switch to the Blue line (towards Raidurg) at Ameerpet station.
    - **From the 🟢 Green Line:** If you're coming via the Green line, switch to the Blue line at Parade Grounds station.
  - For a detailed view of the Hyderabad Metro network, you can [download the metro map here](/images/networkMap.jpg).
