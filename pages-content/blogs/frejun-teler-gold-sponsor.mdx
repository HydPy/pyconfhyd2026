FreJun Teler is a programmable voice infrastructure platform built for
developers creating AI-powered voice applications. It enables AI agents and
applications to make and receive real phone calls using real-time audio
streaming and SIP connectivity.

With low-latency media streaming, global number provisioning, and full programmable call control, Teler simplifies the telecom layer so developers can focus on building intelligent voice experiences. Whether you're building AI voice agents, conversational IVRs, or embedding voice into SaaS products, Teler provides the infrastructure to connect AI directly to live phone calls, without telecom complexity.

We’re excited to support the Python community and developers building the next generation of AI voice systems. Learn more at
[frejun.ai](https://frejun.ai/).

We are immensely grateful to FreJun Teler for their generous support.
